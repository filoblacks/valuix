Method

Six steps from raw financials to a scored deal

  1. 01

    Choose a sector preset

    Select a sector to load industry weights and the EV/EBITDA range used for winsorization.

    Hover EV/EBITDA to see the sector range.

  2. 02

    Load a demo or enter data

    Pick a company from the demo list or type your own numbers. Currency follows the selector.

    Leave Market Cap empty to auto-calculate price × shares.

  3. 03

    Complete core metrics

    Fill debt, cash, EBITDA and revenue FY and FY-3. Capex and OCF give cash conversion; NOPAT and invested capital give ROIC.

    The alerts panel flags missing or invalid inputs.

  4. 04

    Tune weights and modes

    Adjust pillar weights to your mandate. Take-private mode treats low liquidity as a bonus rather than a penalty.

    The pillar breakdown shows effective weights.

  5. 05

    Generate and review

    Review EV/EBITDA, margin, CAGR, net debt, cash conversion and ROIC alongside the weighted pillar bars.

  6. 06

    Export and save

    Print a clean one-pager or a full memo as PDF. Save and load keep your session in this browser.

FAQ

Common questions

Does the data stay local?+

Yes. Everything runs client-side and nothing is sent to a server. Saved sessions live in this browser's local storage.

Can I change the weights?+

Yes, by sector preset or manually. The breakdown always shows the effective weights after renormalization.

EV/EBITDA for luxury is high. How is that handled?+

Each sector carries its own range and winsorization bound, so a 40× multiple is read against luxury peers rather than against industrials.

What does take-private mode do?+

It inverts the liquidity pillar: low average daily volume becomes a bonus instead of a penalty, and the liquidity discount on the total score is switched off.

Can I work in USD or GBP?+

Yes. The currency selector reformats every monetary figure consistently; no conversion is applied to the numbers you enter.
